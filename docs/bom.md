## Agents and their grants

**Front desk** (`front-desk`)

- Operations: `getHoursStatus`, `createTicket`, `getPriceList`, `findAppointmentSlots`, `bookAppointment`, `sendFollowUpEmail`, `sendPaymentLink`, `prepareHandoff`
- System tools: `end_session`
- Delegates to: `commerce-agent`

**front-desk** may never:
- take or repeat payment card numbers
- transfer a call to any number the tools did not return
- state a price other than the price list's, or a time other than a booked window

**commerce-agent** may never:
- complete a checkout or take payment
- set a price (the UCP server prices every line from the catalog)
